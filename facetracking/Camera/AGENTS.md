# Camera integration guidance

- `CameraSessionService` exclusively configures and starts/stops its single `AVCaptureSession` on `cameraQueue`; frame callbacks use the separate serial `outputQueue`.
- Commands carry reducer session IDs and are enqueued in effect order. Invalidate reducer generations before teardown, and never accept a callback as authority to revive an obsolete generation.
- `CameraPreviewSession` is an immutable reference wrapper marked `@unchecked Sendable`: the camera queue mutates session configuration/running state, while MainActor only attaches that same session to `AVCaptureVideoPreviewLayer`.
- Preview is portrait, aspect-fill, and mirrored. Video-data output is portrait and explicitly unmirrored; plan 04 must preserve this one-mirror policy in its transform snapshot.
- `FrameTransformSnapshot` names raw top-left buffer pixels, oriented top-left image pixels, Vision lower-left normalized bounds, preview points, and mirrored top-left normalized viewport coordinates. It carries geometry revision, raw/oriented sizes, quarter-turn, Vision orientation policy, clean aperture, viewport, portrait interface orientation, and mirroring.
- Use `FrameCoordinateMapper.mapVisionBounds` only for already-oriented Vision rectangles. Use `mapRawSamplePoint` for raw luma cell centers; it applies clean-aperture offset and raw rotation before the shared aspect-fill/mirror transform. Missing or inconsistent snapshots return unavailable values.
- Plan 03 requests a physically rotated data output. When delivered pixels are confirmed upright, snapshot them with `.degrees0` plus `.bufferAlreadyOriented`; never also ask Vision to rotate. If delivery is not physically oriented, record the actual quarter-turn and `.visionAppliesSnapshotRotation` instead.
- `FrameAnalyzer` owns synchronous Vision work on the serial output queue. It reuses one revision-3 face-rectangle request, permits one in-flight call, and converts radians to degrees once only when yaw, pitch, and roll are all present.
- Snapshot session ID, geometry revision, viewport, delivered rotation, clean aperture, and mirroring at delegate arrival. Pixel buffers and Vision observations remain within the synchronous callback; only owned scalar observations may leave it.
- `ObservationMailbox` is capacity one/newest-wins and explicitly scheduled. Observation delivery is lossy by design; detector failures and lifecycle events use the separate reliable camera event path.
- Delegate-arrival and Vision-result times are monotonic milliseconds. Presentation timestamps are retained separately only for aggregate diagnostics; never subtract those clocks without R05 evidence.
- Stop invalidates analyzer generation and mailbox before `stopRunning`. Completion must recheck the captured context and discard stale work.
- Keep only front wide-angle video input plus one video-data output. Do not add rear fallback, audio, photo, movie, recording, persistence, or participant imagery diagnostics.
- Negotiate only 8-bit bi-planar YCbCr, preferring full range and falling back to video range. Any other delivered format/layout makes lighting unavailable; never add per-frame RGB/UIImage conversion.
- `SparseLumaSampler` locks plane zero read-only and unlocks with `defer`. Honor actual plane width, height, and stride; sample at most the fixed 64×64 grid and never read row padding.
- Luma sampling occurs inside `FrameAnalyzer.analyze` after the immutable transform and raw face are created and before callback return. Map continuous raw cell centers through that exact snapshot; never retain the pixel buffer, base address, or borrowed pointer.
- The sampler and reusable histogram workspace are owned by the serial analyzer. Only immutable `FaceLightingMetrics` and aggregate format/layout diagnostics may cross the callback boundary.
- Focused tests: `SparseLumaSamplerTests`, `LightingMeasurementTests`, `FrameAnalyzerTests`, and `FrameCoordinateMapperTests`. Physical format/range, pose, latency, clean-aperture, and crop evidence remain device gates.
- `AGENTS.md` must remain outside app resources and bundles.
