## Changes and contracts

- Added pure `Tracking/LightingRules.swift`. It classifies validated metrics into unknown, acceptable, too-dark, too-bright, uneven with optional side, or high-contrast using the versioned `ios-provisional-v1` entry/exit bands.
- Lighting history separates active state from a candidate keyed by assessment plus side. It rejects non-increasing timestamps unchanged, resets immediately for invalid/ineligible input, and resets while discarding a sample after a gap over 300 ms. Warnings require 400 ms; acceptable requires 700 ms.
- Acceptable compact evidence retains the original candidate timestamp and reaches compact only on fresh observation evidence at 1,500 ms. Lighting remains advisory and never changes the positioning hold predicate.
- Added `Tracking/GuidanceRules.swift` and exposed it through `CaptureViewState.guidance`. `CaptureGuidanceProjection` supplies typed primary banner guidance, optional badge assessment/advice/presentation, initial-mask and return-guide flags, and the raw following oval. It contains no localized strings or SwiftUI.
- Positioning corrections outrank all lighting. Active warnings replace only hold/follow guidance. Badge projection requires a current raw face and no camera failure; correction/unknown states are icon-only, while known hold/follow states expand until acceptable compacts.
- Updated `Tracking/AGENTS.md` and `facetrackingTests/AGENTS.md` with the active/candidate, gap-discard, evidence-origin, guidance-priority, and continuous-fixture contracts. No plan 08 UI or localization was implemented.
